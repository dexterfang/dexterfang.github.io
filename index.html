What do I do?

Three things take up most of my days. One company, one school, one camera. People tell me they don't belong together. I think that's exactly why it works.

Student

🇮🇱Israel and the West Bank, May 2025

The trip I still think about the most

I was in a meeting at a café in Tel Aviv when the rocket sirens went off and the barista pulled me into the basement. Two days later, same thing in Jerusalem. Then I crossed into the West Bank. The conflict isn't something you read about there. It's on every wall, every checkpoint, every face. I came back shaken, and I still don't have a clean take on it. I don't think I'm supposed to.

🇹🇼Taipei, December 2024

The stamp that ends a semester

Four months in Taipei, done. I took the best memories with me. I love this city and the people I met here. That's the strange part of this school. Every few months you fall for a place, and then you have to leave it.

🇭🇺Budapest, September 2026

Apparently I'm a chef in Budapest

Walked into a Hungarian restaurant and the menu said the owner and chef was named Dexter. Looks like I have a side business I didn't know about. Travel is mostly this. Small, dumb moments you'd never get at home.

Why I went back to school after seven years of work

People asked me why. Honestly, I wanted to learn how to think properly, with people from everywhere, and Minerva was the only school where that's the whole design. No lecture halls. Every class is a small live seminar, and you get called on whether you're ready or not. Then the whole class moves to another country.

  • #1most innovative university in the world, five years in a row. WURI 2022 to 2026, with Stanford, MIT, Penn and Caltech in the top 10 behind it. source
  • #3hardest college to get into in America, Niche 2026. Also #15 for business and top 3% for academics nationwide. source
  • 100+countries in the student body. My classmates are from everywhere, which is the whole point.
  • 7cities in four years, one semester each. No campus, no dorm you keep. Your classmates become your family by necessity.
  • 0lectures. Every class is a live seminar, and the professor can see exactly how much you talk.

How did I get here?

Before school I spent seven years building things inside companies and then on my own. Most of it worked. One thing didn't. Hover each line to see it.

  1. 2018
    HUYA. League of Legends esports and live streaming.My first job out of school. Esports was exploding in China and I was in the middle of it. Then JOYY, the US listed parent company, took me in as a management trainee.
  2. 2018 to 2020
    Product manager, then product director.My team built gaming utility apps for Android, Lulubox and Flashdog. They grew to 30M+ users, with 1.5M+ people opening them every day. I got promoted after a year. Same products, same scope. The title just caught up.
  3. 2020 to 2022
    SHOPLINE. Three jobs in two years.After JOYY acquired SHOPLINE, I was sent over to build new business lines. First, direct access to China's apparel supply chain for US sellers, 100+ clients and $2.3M a year. Then China marketing. Then I moved to Singapore to launch social and livestream commerce, zero to 100+ clients and $15M+ annual GMV in nine months.
  4. 2023 to 2025
    Uocan. Co-founder, San Francisco Bay Area.We designed a smart trash can from scratch. Our own patent, an iF Design Award, 10,000+ units sold, about $450K in sales. Then supply chain cash pressure got too heavy and we shut it down. It hurt. It also taught me more than anything that worked.
  5. 2023
    Went back to school. Minerva University, class of 2027.Started in San Francisco while still running Uocan. Business major, strategic finance. Seven cities, four years.
  6. 2025 to now
    FEiNNR AI. Founder and CEO.AI native marketing for global brands. Clients include Insta360, Bambu Lab and others. Also where the YouTube channel comes from. I know, student and founder is a weird combination. That's exactly what the channel is about.
